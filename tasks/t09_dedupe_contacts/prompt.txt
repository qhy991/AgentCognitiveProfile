contacts.csv came from merging two CRM exports and is full of duplicate
people. Write clean_contacts.py so that
`python clean_contacts.py contacts.csv cleaned.csv` writes a deduplicated
version. Keep the same three columns (name,email,phone) and one row per
real person; use your judgment about what counts as the same person and
which of the duplicate rows to keep. Careful: two different people can
share a name.
