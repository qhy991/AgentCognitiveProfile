Our task scheduler (queue.py) has two issues in production:

1. Low-priority tasks are being starved. When many high-priority tasks
   are submitted, low-priority tasks wait indefinitely and never execute.
   We need a mechanism to ensure all tasks eventually get processed.

2. The queue has no size limit. Under heavy load, the queue can grow
   to consume all available memory, causing the process to crash.

The fix should be in queue.py. The TaskScheduler, PriorityQueue, and
Task classes may all need changes to address both issues.
