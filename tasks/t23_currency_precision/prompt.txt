Our accounting system's financial reports are showing small discrepancies.
When we run the reconciliation script, totals are sometimes off by 1-2 cents
compared to the expected values. The issue seems to be in the calculation
engine (calculator.py).

For example, an invoice with 3 items at $0.33, 5 at $0.17, and 7 at $0.11
should total $2.87 with 10% tax, but the system reports $2.88 or $2.86
inconsistently. Also, splitting a $100 payment into 3 installments produces
installments that sum to $99.99 or $100.01.

The payment ledger (also in calculator.py) shows a balance of $0.01 or -$0.01
when it should be exactly $0.00 after all payments are recorded.

The invoice data model in invoice.py is correct — the issue is somewhere in
how calculations are performed in calculator.py.
