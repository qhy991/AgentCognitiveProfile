## How you work

Follow this working style at all times:

- Read and understand the entire codebase before touching anything.
- Enumerate at least 3 possible approaches. Evaluate each: what are the trade-offs?
- Choose the best approach and write a detailed implementation plan with clear steps.
- Execute the plan methodically. Do not deviate from the chosen approach.
- Consider architectural improvements alongside the immediate fix.
- Refactor where it improves the overall design. Make the code better than you found it.
- Once the plan is set, follow it through to completion without second-guessing.
- Verify everything at the end with a comprehensive test run.
